\section{Automatic Progressive Overload Algorithm}

\subsection{Mathematical Formulations for 1RM Estimation}

To quantify relative strength in a set of $r$ repetitions executed with load $w$ (expressed in $kg$) at a perceived exertion of $RPE \in [6.0, 10.0]$, we first calculate equivalent Repetitions in Reserve ($RIR$):
\begin{equation}
    RIR = 10.0 - RPE
\end{equation}

Estimated repetitions to absolute failure $r_{\text{max}}$ are calculated as:
\begin{equation}
    r_{\text{max}} = r + RIR
\end{equation}

We use a weighted combination of classic non-linear models by \textbf{Epley} and \textbf{Brzycki}:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Epley Formula:}
    \begin{equation}
        1RM_{\text{Epley}} = w \cdot \left(1 + \frac{r_{\text{max}}}{30}\right)
    \end{equation}

    \item \textbf{Brzycki Formula:}
    \begin{equation}
        1RM_{\text{Brzycki}} = w \cdot \frac{36}{37 - r_{\text{max}}}
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

The final estimated set 1RM ($1RM_{\text{est}}$) is computed by averaging both models:
\begin{equation}
    1RM_{\text{est}} = \frac{1RM_{\text{Epley}} + 1RM_{\text{Brzycki}}}{2}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Effective Volume and Automatic Progressive Overload}

The \textbf{Total Effective Volume} ($V_{\text{effective}}$) of a session for a given exercise, considering working sets only ($is\_warmup = 0$), is expressed as:
\begin{equation}
    V_{\text{effective}} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i \cdot r_i \cdot \alpha(RPE_i)
\end{equation}

where $\alpha(RPE_i)$ represents the fatigue weighting factor:
\begin{equation}
    \alpha(RPE) = \begin{cases}
        1.0 & \text{if } RPE \ge 8.0 \\
        0.85 & \text{if } 7.0 \le RPE < 8.0 \\
        0.70 & \text{if } RPE < 7.0
    \end{cases}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Next Session Load Progression Rule}

The recommended load $w_{\text{next}}$ for the subsequent session is adjusted based on session average $RIR_{\text{avg}}$ and target repetition range $[R_{\text{min}}, R_{\text{max}}]$ completion:

\begin{equation}
    w_{\text{next}} = \begin{cases}
        w_{\text{current}} + \Delta w & \text{if } r_i \ge R_{\text{max}} \;\forall i \quad \text{and} \quad RIR_{\text{avg}} \ge 1.5 \\
        w_{\text{current}} & \text{if } R_{\text{min}} \le r_i < R_{\text{max}} \quad \text{or} \quad 0.5 \le RIR_{\text{avg}} < 1.5 \\
        w_{\text{current}} \cdot (1 - \beta) & \text{if } RIR_{\text{avg}} < 0.5 \quad \text{(deload or excessive fatigue)}
    \end{cases}
\end{equation}

where $\Delta w$ is the standard weight increment ($2.5\,kg$ for compound lifts, $1.25\,kg$ for isolation exercises) and $\beta \in [0.05, 0.10]$ is the fatigue deload factor.
